## Approach
Represent furniture with intrinsic dimensions, transform, appearance, and asset references. Apply uniform resize by default, keep rotation explicit, and calculate overlap/bounds diagnostics without preventing experimentation.
## Non-goals
No 3D models, cloud catalog, persistence implementation, AI estimation, or automatic photo extraction.
## Acceptance
Users can create/catalog furniture, attach images, place and manipulate it in 2D, preserve proportions, and see actionable collision and room-fit feedback.
