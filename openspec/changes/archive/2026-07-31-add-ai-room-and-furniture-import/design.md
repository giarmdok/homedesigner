## Approach
Define provider-neutral asynchronous adapters for room detection and furniture estimation. Keep keys out of source control, send only explicit user-selected assets, present confidence and proposed geometry/dimensions for review, then apply through existing commands. Failures, timeouts, missing keys, and low confidence return to manual workflows.
## Non-goals
No autonomous publishing, mandatory AI, secret committing, provider lock-in, or replacement of manual correction.
## Acceptance
Users can configure a key safely, submit selected photos, review confidence-tagged suggestions, correct/reject them, and continue editing when AI is unavailable or wrong.
